#   - The part after | is optional: it's a longer label used only on the sitemap.
#   - Lines starting with ":" are sitemap-only headings (e.g. ": Thesis").
#   - A page listed in two places: start the extra listing with "= ".
#     Its sidebar follows the listing without "=".
#   - "~ " at the start of a line: show it on the sitemap but not in sidebars.
#   - Lines starting with "#" are notes and are ignored.

# After editing, run:   python3 wiki/build.py
