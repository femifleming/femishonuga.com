Information Adjustment and the Cybernetics of Afrofuturism, 2025-Current

~> LISTEN HERE

Information Adjustment and the Cybernetics of Afrofuturism is an ongoing series of multichannel audio compositions and live performance centered around afro-cybernetics and dream state sonification. These compositions explore an anthology of sound related to the black experience and extend them into imaged black futures through synthesis, sampling, processing and spatialization techniques. The sonic collection is informed by field recordings, logging dreams and continuous experimentation. Through improvisation as a ritual practice, I explore the cybernetic natures of electronic systems as extensions of the body. Sonic palettes include but are not limited to: bells, horns, ambient drones, field recordings, impossible architectural spaces and abrasion in the physical and mental space. The work investigates how composed soundscapes can become mechanisms for mediation surrounding these imagined futures, and navigate afropessimist realities. The work is grounded in giving thanks and acknowledgement to the ground beneath our feet.

The most recent composition was commissioned by Sonic Acts, for their 2026 Biennial Show, and composed for playback on their 8.2 channel speaker array at Zone2Source.

"Information Adjustment and the Cybernetics of Afrofuturism by Femi Shonuga-Fleming moves like a river – full of slow eddies and sudden surges – through space and imagined moss floors. The work traces nonlinear histories of Black and Indigenous organic technology, grounding bodies through deep listening as ritual. Drums pulse; jazz loosens into continuous kaizen, transcendence accumulating through interruption and return. Field recordings and synthesised tones hold vibration like breath, with the speakers functioning as ritual objects." - Sonic Acts

The first composition for this series was installed at Residual Noise: Spatial Audio Concert #2 at Ambisonic Cube, Main Hall, The Lindemann Performing Arts Center, Providence, RI. The piece was composed in Reaper for playback on the 43.4 speaker array.

"Femi Shonuga-Fleming used ambisonics and spatial audio to craft abstract sonic worlds rooted in Afrofuturism and ancestral memory. The piece, 'Information Retrieval and the Cybernetics of Afrofuturism,' invited deep listening as a ritual, honoring both future possibilities and Indigenous groundings."
