Of Sound and Vision

Multichannel Performance
Duration: 00:09:34
Components: Serge Modular Synth, Max MSP, Eurorack Modular Synths, Elektron Samplers and Drum Machines, IEM Plugins, Reaper, CRT Televisions
Location: RISD SRST (Studio for Research in Sound and Technology), Providence RI

RISD Wintersession 2021

Performance for Of Sound and Vision Wintersession Course in collaboration with Haram Lee

Using the Serge Modular Synthesizer, My personal Eurorack Modular Synth, Max MSP, Reaper, The 25.4 Speaker Array and the Elektron Monomachine, Machinedrum and Octatrack

I created a patch in Max that used the camera to track hand movements and map them to CV outs via ADAT to control filters, timing and envelopes on the Serge modular synth.
