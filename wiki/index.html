Sound Work


Femi is a New York based sound artist whose compositions explore the intersections of acousmatics and cultural anthropology. His work employs site specific rituals that investigate environments and create new forms of discourse in conversation with the resulting research. Through performance, multichannel installation and sculpture, he examines the dynamic relationships between human gesture and sound technology. Femi works with handmade electro-acoustic instruments and generative systems as cybernetic mediums, extending embodied experience into spatial interventions. The focus of his work is to amplify the world of the surreal by designing new immersive environments. He is particularly interested in sonic acts as ritual and the poetics of sound technology. His work has been presented at the Sonic Acts Biennial in Amsterdam (2026), Waterworks Festival in Massachusetts (2024), and Ende Tymes in New York (2022), among other venues and festivals.
