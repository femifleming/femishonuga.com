Performance

This performance is an exploration of identities as sonic profiles, and giving these sonic profiles an immediate analog visualization through the technology of the CRT Television. These identities are stretched and abstracted through granular synthesis, cybernetic feedback and generative synthesis systems. This performance is a reflection of the control and no-control over generative systems, creating new architectural environments through experimental and improvisational composition. When they pulse back and forth is the astable multivibrator, or the train signaling.
